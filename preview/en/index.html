03 / A little about meBACKGROUND / PROFILE

Research is not only about finding a result; it is also about understanding why an idea fails.

My background is in actuarial science and quantitative modeling. I'm interested in exploring what happens when academic ideas are put into practice: working with real data, building systems, facing practical constraints, and understanding why some strategies work while others fail. My goal is to bring research closer to concrete investment decisions, where results are measured not only by statistical metrics but also by their ability to generate returns and preserve capital.
